# One Time Handshake (OTH) — Investor & Business Model Specification

## Market Opportunity

The Global Identity and Access Management (IAM) market is projected to reach **$34.5 Billion by 2030**. SMS-based authentication costs enterprises billions in telecom gateway fees ($0.03 to $0.15 per SMS OTP) while offering poor security against SIM swapping and intercept attacks.

## Target Customer Segments

1. **Government & Public Sector**: Border security, military communications, rural subsidy distribution (PDS), and national digital ID systems.
2. **Banking & Fintech**: High-value transfer verification, ATM cardless transactions, and offline rural banking agents.
3. **Enterprise & Critical Infrastructure**: Power grids, defense contractors, healthcare networks, and remote workforce authentication.

## Business & Monetization Model

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       OTH Revenue Streams                         |

```
